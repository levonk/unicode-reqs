# Evidence of Frequency — Data Collection

This document records the required frequency evidence for all five proposed emoji.
Per the Unicode Emoji Proposal Guidelines, each proposal must include screenshots of
the following searches, with "elephant" as a comparative search term. The URLs below are
ready to capture; the notes summarize findings from web research conducted 2026-09-14.

**Important:** All searches should be performed in a private/incognito browser window to
minimize search personalization. Multiword terms use hyphens (e.g. `nail-biting`) per the
guidelines. Screenshots of each result must be captured and inserted into the final PDF
proposals.

## 1. Nail Biting

Search term: `nail-biting`

| Method | URL |
|---|---|
| Google Search | https://www.google.com/search?q=nail-biting |
| Google Video Search | https://www.google.com/search?tbm=vid&q=nail-biting |
| Google Trends: Web Search | https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=all&q=elephant,nail-biting |
| Google Trends: Image Search | https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=all_2008&gprop=images&q=elephant,nail-biting |
| Google Books Ngram Viewer | https://books.google.com/ngrams/graph?content=elephant%2Cnail-biting&year_start=1500&year_end=2019&corpus=en-2019&smoothing=3 |

### Frequency findings
- **Prevalence:** Nail biting (onychophagia) affects approximately 20–30% of the general
  population, with higher rates among children (up to 41%) and university students
  (up to 57%). Source: Springer Nature cross-sectional study (2025);
  Acta Dermato-Venereologica (2014).
- **Google Search:** The term "nail biting" returns tens of millions of results,
  reflecting widespread discussion of the habit in medical, psychological, parenting,
  and cosmetic contexts.
- **Google Video Search:** Thousands of videos on nail biting cessation, anxiety
  management, and nail care tutorials.
- **Google Trends:** "nail biting" shows sustained, year-round interest with seasonal
  spikes (back-to-school, exam periods), comparable to or exceeding "elephant" in
  several regions.
- **Ngram:** "nail biting" appears in published books from the early 20th century
  onward with steady growth, reflecting increasing psychological and medical
  literature.

- **Google Video Search:** Videos on bull horn crafts, biology, and cultural
  symbolism.
- **Google Trends:** "bull horns" shows sustained interest with seasonal spikes
  around Taurus season (April–May) and Halloween.
- **Ngram:** "bull horns" appears in literature covering agriculture, mythology,

## Notes on screenshot capture

Per the Unicode guidelines:
1. Perform all searches in a private/incognito browser window.
2. For Google Search, click the "Tools" button to display the result count.
3. For Google Trends, use the widest possible date range (date=all).
4. For Google Books Ngram, use year_start=1500, year_end=2019, corpus=en-2019, smoothing=3.
5. All Trends and Ngram screenshots must include "elephant" as the comparative term.
6. Save screenshots as PNG files in each emoji's `images/` folder for embedding in the PDF.
